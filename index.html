About

TalentScreen is a demo recruitment screening tool that parses candidate CVs (PDF/DOCX/TXT), extracts work history, calculates tenure, detects company sectors, and ranks candidates against a job description.

This demo performs client-side keyword-based scoring. For production-grade accuracy, connect the scoreCandidate() and extractText() functions to your FastAPI backend with LLM reranking.
